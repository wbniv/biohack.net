# 2025 U.S. federal return: extension deadline

Verified against IRS sources on **7 August 2026** for a calendar-year U.S. individual income-tax return.

## Result

If the filed extension was **Form 4868**, the filing deadline for the 2025 return is **15 October 2026**.

The overseas rules are easy to misread:

1. A qualifying U.S. citizen or resident alien abroad on the ordinary due date automatically receives two additional months, through **15 June 2026**.
2. A timely Form 4868 extends the filing deadline to **15 October 2026**. It does not add six months after 15 June.
3. An extension to file is not an extension to pay. The ordinary payment deadline still governs, and unpaid tax can accrue interest and possibly penalties.

## Approval-record caveat

No IRS approval notice, Form 4868 acceptance, or Form 2350 approval was found in the workspace. Before relying on the date, save the actual extension confirmation and identify the form:

- **Form 4868:** use **15 October 2026**.
- **Form 2350:** use the exact date approved on Form 2350. This is a special extension for a taxpayer who needs more time to qualify for the foreign earned-income or housing exclusion under the bona-fide-residence or physical-presence test; it is not the ordinary six-month filing extension.
- **Separate written extension beyond six months:** use the date on the IRS approval. Certain taxpayers living abroad can request additional time, but it is not automatic merely because they are overseas.

## Evidence to retain

- extension-form type;
- electronic acceptance or stamped/approved notice;
- submission date and confirmation number;
- filed-return acceptance;
- payment confirmation and the date paid.

## Planning deadlines

- **1 October 2026 — internal:** locate the extension acceptance, establish which form controls, finish and review the return, and resolve any missing-document or payment issue.
- **15 October 2026 — IRS:** file the return and retain its acceptance, unless a Form 2350 approval states a different controlling date.
