Return to Thailand — January 2027 master plan

The outcome (the visa is a means, not the goal)

Be back in Bangkok with the cat around January 2027, on a durable status appropriate for actually living there, while genuinely learning Thai — without making the unnecessary Thailand → USA → Thailand loop.

There is no preferred visa category yet. The route is whichever has the strongest approval case after written answers, document checks, and a consultation with Integrity Legal:

  • DTV workcation — real app-development portfolio and financial evidence.
  • DTV cooking — genuine qualifying course with the provider packet the deciding mission expects.
  • Non‑ED language — genuine Thai study, either applied for from Viet Nam or through TM.87 after returning to Bangkok.

Learning Thai is unconditional. It happens under all three routes. Under Non‑ED it is both the real activity and the visa basis; under either DTV it remains the real activity but is independent of the visa basis.

Decision rule: prepare three truthful, documentable cases; ask Integrity Legal to assess their practical approval odds; then select the strongest route. Optimize for approval probability, not convenience or theoretical elegance. Thai study is not part of that tradeoff; it continues whichever legal wrapper wins.

Route geography

Teal = the working outbound route: Bangkok → Seoul/Incheon → Da Nang. The return to Bangkok happens only after a decision: on the issued status, or visa-exempt after refusal to execute TM.87. While the application is pending, remain in Viet Nam under the 90-day visa. Amber dashed = the triggered-only cat-export road fallback toward HCMC.

Calendar — deadlines, gates, and where you are

Solid markers are deadlines or committed travel. Rings are decision gates or target dates. The country ribbon is the working itinerary, not a visa requirement. The USA is deliberately absent: it returns only as a contingency if the Asia-status plan fails.

Definition of done

This plan succeeds when all four are true:

  • You and the cat are back in Bangkok without an unnecessary Pacific crossing.
  • You hold DTV or have a timely, documented Non‑ED/TM.87 application in progress.
  • Thai classes are enrolled and begun for their own value.
  • The 2026 Thai day count remains below 180 and the official evidence is retained.
